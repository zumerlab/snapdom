SnapStudio privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

SnapStudio is a browser extension that captures parts of web pages. It is made by Zumerlab and built on SnapDOM.

What SnapStudio collects

Nothing. SnapStudio has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not collect, store or send your personal data, your browsing history or the content of the pages you visit.

What happens on your device

  • Captures. When you capture something, SnapStudio reads that part of the page inside your browser and produces the file there. The result goes to your downloads or your clipboard, where you asked for it.
  • History. What you copy or save is kept in a list of recent captures inside the extension, on your device, so you can get it again. It holds up to 40 captures. You can delete one, delete all, or turn History off in Preferences. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • Settings. Your options, preferences and saved recipes are kept in the extension's local storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.
  • Images from other sites. If you allow SnapStudio on every site, it can download images that the page shows but cannot read by itself, so they appear in your capture. They are requested directly from the site that hosts them, the same way your browser already did to display them, and are used only for that capture.
  • Fonts. To draw icon fonts correctly, a capture may download the font file the page already uses (for example Material Icons from Google Fonts). That request goes to the font's host and carries nothing about you or the page.

Permissions

SnapStudio gets access to a tab when you open it from the toolbar on that tab. Access to every site is optional and is requested only if you press "Allow on every site". You can withdraw it at any time from the extension's details in chrome://extensions.
