PDF and vector export for SnapDOM.

Turn a SnapDOM capture into a searchable PDF or editable vector artwork. Both plugins are now part of the SnapDOM monorepo under the MIT license.

snapdom-pdf

Export a faithful capture with selectable, searchable text.

The visible page comes from SnapDOM's capture. A real PDF text layer adds selection, search, copy and working links without re-laying out the content. Paginate onto A4 with headers, footers and page numbers.

snapdom-vector

Export DOM captures as editable vector documents.

Create shapes, editable text and real layers for print, plotters, Illustrator, Penpot and Figma. Every approximation is listed, with what changed and why.

PDF and Vector are available on npm in @zumer/snapdom-plugins 4.x and require SnapDOM 3.x. PDF and Vector have their own source and test folders inside packages/plugins.
