SnapDOM Vector

You're in.

Polar is emailing your receipt. Three steps get the plugin into your hands, and the first one needs a click.

  1. Connect your GitHub account

    Your receipt links to your Polar customer portal. Open it and connect GitHub next to your SnapDOM Vector subscription. That is how Polar knows who to invite, so nothing arrives until it is done.

  2. Accept the invitation to snapdom-pro/vector

    GitHub emails it to the address on your account, and it also waits at github.com/snapdom-pro/vector/invitations. Releases and issues live in that repository.

    This one expires. GitHub invitations time out after seven days, and they land in spam more often than they should. If yours never arrived or ran out, reconnect GitHub from the portal, or write to us and we will send it again.

  3. Download the release

    The repository's Releases page has the ZIP: the minified build, the readable one, the TypeScript declarations, the separate PDF backend, the Figma plugin and the licence. Watch the repository's releases to hear about new versions.

Your subscription renews every year until you cancel it from the same portal. While it is active you get new releases, the repository and support. The versions you already downloaded stay yours to use after it ends.
