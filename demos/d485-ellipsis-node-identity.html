#485 — truncation measurement must not replace live text nodes

snapDOM bakes text-overflow: ellipsis and -webkit-line-clamp by measuring the live element: it writes a candidate string, reads scrollWidth/scrollHeight and binary-searches the cut. Doing that through textContent destroys the element's text nodes; React/Vue/Svelte keep references to them and later crash with NotFoundError: Failed to execute 'removeChild'. The measurement now writes textNode.data, which keeps node identity. "Remove first item" below is what a framework does on the next render: it must not throw after a capture.
