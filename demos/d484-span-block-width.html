#484 — <span> with display:block and an authored width

Width softening drops a content-bearing span's used width and re-adds it as a min-width floor. That only reproduces the box when the width is auto and the box is shrink-to-fit: case 1 stretched to 100% of the container and case 2 (flex item, no floor by #406) collapsed to its text. The two controls must keep softening — that is what keeps #429 and the max-content "Timestamp" case from re-wrapping under font fallback.
