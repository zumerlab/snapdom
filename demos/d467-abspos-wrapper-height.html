#467 — a wrapper whose only children are position:absolute loses its height

.hero's 160px height comes from a stylesheet, and its only child is an absolutely-positioned overlay. hasFlowFast used to trust textContent (sees text inside the abspos child) and scrollHeight (floored at clientHeight, never drops even when nothing is really in flow) — both signals lied, so the height got stripped and .hero collapsed to 0, letting .after paint over it. Before the fix: no purple/pink gradient band, the dark "below the hero" bar sits right at the top.
