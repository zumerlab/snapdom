Summary of the University Students' Innovation and Employment Empowerment Conference

This event is an empowerment lecture focusing on the career development and innovative employment of college students in the digital era. The conference integrates industry trend analysis, career planning guidance, and enterprise talent demands. Through tools such as the Four Skills Model, it explores the enhancement of job-seeking competitiveness and interview skills. The guests provided multi-dimensional employment solutions for college students from perspectives of entrepreneurial practice, educational empowerment, and psychology.
